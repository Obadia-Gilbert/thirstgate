Privacy policy

Your data stays on your iPhone

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short: Thirstgate has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no third-party tracking. What you log is kept on your iPhone, and the camera check never records or sends anything. We don’t collect your personal data, so we have none to sell or share.

This policy explains how the Thirstgate iPhone app (“Thirstgate”, “we”, “us”), provided by Obadia Mwakyusa, handles information. It applies to the app and to this website.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Thirstgate saves the following on your device only:

  • Your settings: daily goal, glass size, minutes each glass unlocks, proof mode and reminder times.
  • The glasses you log: the time, the amount and how it was checked.
  • Daily totals, streaks, streak freezes and the milestones you’ve reached.
  • The apps and categories you chose to lock (see Screen Time below).

This information is stored in the app’s private storage and shared only with Thirstgate’s own extensions on the same device, which draw the lock screen and re-lock apps on time. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Camera

If you turn on a camera check, Thirstgate uses the front camera while the drink screen is open to check that a glass reaches your lips and its water level drops. This is done on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in Vision framework. Each frame is analysed in memory and discarded straight away. No photos, video or face data are recorded, saved or transmitted, and no face is identified or recognised. The only thing kept is whether the check passed.

Screen Time

Thirstgate uses Apple’s Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to lock the apps you choose. Apple only gives Thirstgate anonymous tokens for your choices; Thirstgate cannot see which apps they are, read your app usage or see your browsing. The locks are applied by iOS on your device.

Notifications

Reminders and “drink to unlock” notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Purchases

Thirstgate Plus is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. The app only receives confirmation from Apple of whether your subscription is active. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, and we use it only to reply to you. We delete support emails when they’re no longer needed, and no later than two years after the conversation ends.

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Deleting your data

Delete Thirstgate from your iPhone and everything it stored is deleted with it, and every lock it set is removed. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; if you’ve emailed us, ask and we’ll delete those emails.

Children

Thirstgate is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since the app collects none, these mainly apply to support emails. Contact us to use them.

Changes

If we change how Thirstgate handles data, we’ll update this page and the date above. If a change means the app would start collecting data, we’ll tell you in the app first.
